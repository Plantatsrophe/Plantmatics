## 1. Executive Summary and Theoretical Framework

I established this protocol to conduct automated high-throughput vegetative phenotyping and herbarium triage across North American ***Packera* Á. Löve & D. Löve** (Asteraceae: Senecioneae) with the taxonomically recalcitrant ***Packera dubia* complex** as my primary dissertation baseline. The lineage presents acute identification challenges on pressed sheets, as developmental heterophylly produces radical shape shifts between juvenile rosettes and mature cauline leaves, whereas manual curatorial annotations frequently confuse sympatric congeners.

I frame morphological diagnosability within the **Unified Species Concept (USC; de Queiroz 2007)** through **Integration by Congruence (Padial et al. 2010)**. Under the USC, species represent separately evolving metapopulation lineages. Lineage divergence generates morphological diagnosability and reproductive isolation alongside ecological divergence and reciprocal monophyly as contingent properties along the speciation continuum. I evaluate automated vegetative shape data alongside five independent lines of evolutionary and infrastructural evidence:

1. **Reproductive Macro-Morphology and Anthesis:** I quantify capitulum aspect ratios and phyllary counts alongside floret dimensions and 3-tier optical anthesis staging.
2. **Cytotaxonomic Cytotypes:** I harvest and cross-link public plus label-derived chromosome counts and C-value measurements ($2n = 44, 46, 88$; Kowal 1975) into Tier A SQLite tables (`cytological_records` paired with `taxon_cytology_summary`) so empirical base numbers and polyploid series test diploid versus tetraploid isolation without circular trait leakage.
3. **Reduced-Representation nextRAD Phylogenomics:** I align morphometric clusters against high-density SNP datasets that resolve reticulate evolution and hybrid swarms across lineage boundaries.
4. **0 to 15 cm Rhizosphere and USGS SGMC Bedrock Stack:** I cache edaphic profiles in SQLite `environmental_cache` and `environmental_rtree` through 0 to 15 cm depth-weighting (`(5 * v_0_5 + 10 * v_5_15) / 15`), where I combine tight `30 to 50 m` focal SSURGO map units (`ssurgo_rock_outcrop_pct` separated from `ssurgo_lithic_series_pct` alongside `ssurgo_bedrock_depth_cm`) with 100 m SOLUS100 grids (`anylithicdpt_cm_p.tif` lithic contact across 19 cached GeoTIFFs), 6-class USGS SGMC lithology (`sgmc_lithology_class`), 30 m USGS 3DEP topography, and Climatic ($D_{\text{clim}}$) versus Edaphic ($D_{\text{edaph}}$) niche equivalency tests (Warren et al. 2008).
5. **Central Relational WAL Architecture (`data/plantmatics.sqlite`):** All pipeline stages transact against a centralized SQLite WAL database (`PLANTMATICS_SCHEMA_USER_VERSION = 20261127`, aliased as `PACKERA_SCHEMA_USER_VERSION`, with automatic atomic zero-loss rename migration from legacy `data/packera_pipeline.sqlite` including `-wal` and `-shm` sidecars) housing Tier A shared tables (`taxon_synonyms`, `taxon_backbone_profiles`, `natureserve_subnational_ranges`, `vouchers`, `plant_individuals`, `extracted_leaves`, `voucher_reproductive_phenology`, `environmental_cache` with `environmental_rtree`, `genomic_samples`, `cytological_records`, plus `taxon_cytology_summary`) and Tier B run-scoped tables (`pipeline_runs`, `run_active_cohort`, `leaf_efa_harmonics`, `otu_morphometrics`, `spatial_rf_predictions`, `genetic_lineage_assignments`, `curation_triage`, plus `taxon_complexes`, with master view `v_integrative_triage_master`). Under schema version `20261127`, Tier A `vouchers` and `v_integrative_triage_master` also expose five Step 01 taxonomic concept clarity columns (`typified_basionym`, `verbatim_taxon_concept`, `concept_synonymy_status`, `identification_year`, plus `is_open_or_hybrid_concept`), while Step 05 exposes `ssurgo_lithic_series_pct` in `environmental_cache` alongside five macroecological clarity columns (`hopkins_adjusted_doy_resid`, `anthesis_dominance_weight`, `allochronic_bimodality_delta_bic`, `nearest_type_niche_basionym`, and `nearest_type_basionym_edaphic_z`) in `spatial_rf_predictions` and `v_integrative_triage_master`. In parallel, the database exposes dynamic Open-Set Recognition distances (`min_congener_mahal_d2`, `min_congener_taxon`, `open_set_novelty_p`), topological Euler Characteristic Transform descriptors (`ECT_PC1..PC3`, `Cauline_ECT_PC1..PC2`, `ect_lobe_complexity`, `cauline_ect_lobe_complexity`, `ect_bilateral_asym`, `heteroblasty_ect_distance`), Confident Learning noise flags (`is_confident_label_noise`), polyploid allele-balance cytotypes and `(h, Q12)` / `gdi` phylogenomic delimitation fields, public *Packera* nuclear backbone and organellar/`waxy` placement metrics (`public_plastome_match`, `public_its_match`, `waxy_lineage_match`, `public_backbone_clade`, `public_phytop_ils_index`, `public_phytop_ih_index`), CoL WCVP `2232` plus BHL API v3 and NatureServe Explorer taxonomic profiles (`backbone_basionym_name`, `protologue_url`, `natureserve_global_rank`), USACE NWPL wetland ratings (`nwpl_indicator_code`, `nwpl_wetland_score`), USGS `DS-801` soil geochemistry plus USFWS NWI metrics (`soil_ca_mg_molar_ratio`, `soil_ultramafic_ni_cr_index`, `soil_carbonate_c_pct`, `is_nwi_wetland`, `nwi_wetland_system`, `nwi_wetland_dist_m`), lineage population cohesion indicators (`cryptic_cluster_cohesion_pass`), four-tier unsequenced genomic propagation columns (`genomic_propagation_tier`, `tier0_calibrated_taxon`, `tier0_calibrated_prob`, ## 2. Phase 1 Protocol: Ingestion, Standardization and US EPA Level III Ecoregions (`01_voucher_harvester.py`)

I designed Phase 1 to solve the foundational challenge of herbarium data intake: raw specimen databases contain corrupted taxonomic names and collector duplicate conflicts alongside geographic clustering that distorts statistical models when records enter uncritically.

* **Darwin Core Schema Contract and Relational UPSERT (`vouchers`):**
  I harvest occurrences into SQLite `vouchers` (PK: `catalogNumber`) via atomic UPSERT while I resolve synonyms through `taxon_synonyms`, and I retain 100% of primary duplicate sheets plus verified nomenclatural types in Tier A with neutral `spatial_balance_weight = 1.0`. I export a synchronized CSV `data/tables/curated_vouchers.csv` built on the 23 core columns in `EXPORT_COLUMNS` (`catalogNumber`, `institutionCode`, `scientificName`, `species_raw`, `recordedBy`, `recordNumber`, `identifiedBy`, `determiner_tier`, `type_status`, `county`, `stateProvince`, `decimalLatitude`, `decimalLongitude`, `coordinateUncertainty`, `year`, `month`, `day`, `eventDate`, `regional_group`, `image_path`, `exsiccatae_key`, `is_primary_duplicate`, plus `duplicate_count` paired with `species_standardized`, `spatial_balance_weight`, `infraspecific_taxon`, and duplicate audit or concept columns) alongside optional concept and duplicate-audit columns (`typified_basionym`, `verbatim_taxon_concept`, `concept_synonymy_status`, `identification_year`, `is_open_or_hybrid_concept`, `infraspecific_taxon`, `exsiccatae_taxonomic_conflict`, plus `exsiccatae_gold_taxon`), whereas `usda_symbol` and `usda_county_status` stay SQLite-only so CSV width never breaks downstream readers. Inside `step01_concept_authority_utils.py`, I also strip botanical autonyms (such as `Packera aurea var. aurea`) and trivial `f.`/`forma` ranks from `infraspecific_taxon` while preserving valid `subsp.` and `var.` trinomials.
* **Herbarium Rooftop and County-Centroid Coordinate Quarantine (`scripts/core/coord_hygiene_utils.py`):**
  During Step 01 ingestion, I classify suspect coordinates into `is_centroid_or_inst_coord` paired with `coord_quarantine_reason` rather than dropping voucher records from morphological or taxonomic curation. Records matching known herbarium institutional coordinates (such as UNC Coker Hall rooftops when paired with municipal locality strings) receive `coord_quarantine_reason = "herbarium_institution_rooftop"`, while records matching US county centroids from `us_spatial_graph` receive `coord_quarantine_reason = "county_centroid_suspect"`. Wild microhabitat locality phrases alongside `is_type_specimen` records remain unflagged so real bedrock outcrops and primary nomenclatural types stay active, and all quarantined vouchers retain `georef_qc_status = "valid"` plus their raw `decimalLatitude`/`decimalLongitude` coordinates across SQLite `vouchers` and `v_integrative_triage_master`. Downstream spatial kernel density weighting in Steps 03 to 05 and Tier B preflight (`compute_spatial_balance_weights()` and `spatially_balance_and_thin_vouchers()`) excludes `is_centroid_or_inst_coord` and unpromoted `Tier_3_Bronze` rows from local density `d_local` while recomputing active anchor weights.
* **Dynamic Taxon Standardization, WCVP `2232` Backbone, and Range Screening:**
  I configure genus and family scope alongside `taxa.synonym_genera`, `taxa.eastern_resident_whitelist`, and `taxa.transcontinental_boreal_taxa` dynamically in `config/config.yaml` and resolve them through `scripts/core/step01_biogeography_utils.py`. I standardize binomials and basionyms dynamically through `expand_taxon_synonyms_gbif()`, paired with `scripts/data_prep/download_taxonomic_backbone.py`, which harvests Catalogue of Life WCVP dataset `2232` accepted names alongside homotypic versus heterotypic synonym arrays, Biodiversity Heritage Library (BHL) API v3 protologue deep-links, and NatureServe Explorer global and subnational ranks into flat `data/taxonomy/` CSVs plus SQLite `taxon_backbone_profiles` and `natureserve_subnational_ranges`. When I omit taxon flags, the harvester queries all species in the active genus through `discover_genus_packera_species_gbif()`, whereas `resolve_eastern_resident_whitelist()` unions YAML residents with native TDWG Level 3 and NatureServe taxa in SQLite (`normalize_packera_orthography()` normalizes `"Packera millefolia"` and `"Packera ×memmingeri"` cleanly), and `filter_vouchers_by_usda_county_range()` falls back automatically to SQLite `natureserve_subnational_ranges` for unmapped post-2000 segregates or Canadian provinces (`natureserve_state_in_range`, `natureserve_exotic_or_waif`, `out_of_range`). When I pass `taxon` or `focal_taxon` CLI flags, the harvester restricts analysis strictly to those specified taxa, and preflight reconciles any requested-taxon vouchers stored in SQLite `vouchers` into the active run cohort via `reconcile_curated_vouchers_csv_with_db()`.
* **Two-Pass `StillImage` Pagination, Ungeoreferenced Rescue, and Type Rescue:**
  I query GBIF with `mediaType="StillImage"` across configured `countries` (default `["US", "CA"]`) using a two-pass country quota allocator: Pass 1 grants each country up to `ceil(max_records_per_taxon / len(countries))` records so US occurrences never starve Canadian collections, and Pass 2 rolls unused capacity back into non-exhausted countries from saved offsets. Under `geographic_scope = "eastern_na"`, I filter Western US states and Western or Northern Canadian provinces (`BC`, `AB`, `SK`, `YT`, `NT`, `NU`) plus longitudes west of `-100.0` deg W, whereas `evaluate_transcontinental_ecoregion_gate()` screens transcontinental boreal taxa (`Packera paupercula`, `Packera indecora`, `Packera pauciflora`) by EPA and CEC Level III ecoregion so boreal forest extensions pass while Great Plains and Cordilleran ecoregions drop. When `include_ungeoreferenced_state_vouchers: true` is active in `config/config.yaml`, I execute an additional `hasCoordinate=False` cis-Mississippi state rescue pass so historical sheets lacking coordinates remain eligible for county-containment ecoregion lookup. In parallel, my unconditional `typeStatus="*"` rescue loop runs across every target country plus an un-filtered `country=None` pass so global historical types hosted in European or un-coded herbaria still enter Tier A even after `max_records_per_taxon` fills.
* **Indumentum-Safe Optical Sharpness QC, Authority-First Deduplication, and WebP Storage:**
  When `check_sharpness: true` is active in `config/config.yaml`, `validate_image_quality()` measures both global central-ROI Laplacian variance and the 90th-percentile Laplacian variance across an `8 x 8` non-overlapping tile grid (`sharpness_score = max(global_lap_var, tile_p90_lap_var)` against `min_sharpness_laplacian: 80.0`), so crisp label or margin detail on densely tomentose leaves passes while blurred or upscaled scans reject as `"blurry_or_upscaled"`. Inside `stratify_duplicates()`, I rank duplicate gatherings by verified type status first and `determiner_tier` ahead of `in_range_rank`, `home_herbarium`, and scan resolution, and I propagate unanimous `exsiccatae_gold_taxon` labels across duplicate sheets even when individual duplicates sit outside native county maps. In Pass 2 of deduplication, records carrying incomplete `-00-00` dates, `unknown` collectors, or `is_centroid_or_inst_coord` coordinates remain unmerged singletons instead of collapsing via `< 50 m` spatial proximity. Full-sheet imagery in `data/raw_vouchers/` and crops in `data/cropped_patches/` default to quality-92 `.webp` compression (`method=4` via PIL), which yields a 70% to 80% disk reduction relative to JPEG or PNG with zero edge degradation.
* **Official US EPA Level III Ecoregion Point-in-Polygon Delineation:**
  I assign `regional_group` strictly through spatial intersection against official **US EPA Level III Ecoregions** (Omernik & Griffith 2014; `data/environmental/epa_ecoregions_l3/`):
  $$\text{regional\_group}_i = \text{Ecoregion}_k \iff (x_i, y_i) \in \mathcal{P}_k$$
* **Ungeoreferenced Specimen Fallback Policy:**
  When coordinate data are missing ($y_i = \text{NA}$), I evaluate the US Census county boundary polygon $\mathcal{C}_i$:
  $$\text{regional\_group}_i = \begin{cases} \text{Ecoregion}_k & \text{if } \frac{\text{Area}(\mathcal{C}_i \cap \mathcal{P}_k)}{\text{Area}(\mathcal{C}_i)} \ge 0.95 \\ \text{NA} & \text{otherwise} \end{cases}$$
* **Nomenclatural Type Hierarchy and Priority Engine:**
  - `PRIMARY_TYPE_STATUSES = {"HOLOTYPE", "LECTOTYPE", "NEOTYPE", "EPITYPE", "ISOTYPE", "ISOLECTOTYPE", "ISONEOTYPE"}` (`Tier_0_Primary_Type`, rank 2).
  - `SECONDARY_TYPE_STATUSES = {"SYNTYPE", "ISOSYNTYPE", "PARATYPE", "ISOPARATYPE", "TYPE", "COTYPE"}` (`Tier_0_Secondary_Type`, rank 1).
  - I resolve fine-grained ICN priority through `classify_nomenclatural_type_subrank()` (`2.2` primary, `2.0` isotype, `1.5` syntype, `1.0` paratype; Turland et al. 2018). I exempt verified types from uncertainty or partial-date filters and retain them unconditionally during spatial thinning and outgroup selection.
* **2-Layer Dynamic Authority Stratification and Concept Clarity (`step01_concept_authority_utils.py`, `parse_determiner_tier`, `apply_corpus_authority_tiers`):**
  - **Layer 1 (`Tier_1_Gold` Core Monographers, Types, and Open/Hybrid Quarantine):** Dynamic authority discovery (`discover_corpus_authorities`) evaluates `identifiedBy` independently of `recordedBy`, and it falls back to `recordedBy` only when `identifiedBy` is blank or explicitly references the collector, which prevents collector-as-determiner false Gold promotions. Non-type `Tier_1_Gold` status is restricted to post-1960 *Packera* revisionists and segregate authors (`CORE_PACKERA_GOLD_SURNAMES` plus `CORE_PACKERA_GOLD_REGEXES`: Barkley, Kowal, Trock, Bain, Mahoney, Soderholm, Mandel, Moore-Pollard, Weakley, Poindexter, Gramling, Boufford, Nesom, Uttal, C. C. Freeman, J. L. Golden, E. R. Moore, L. D. Estes or Dwayne Estes, W. A. Weber, and J. G. Packer), whereas bare common surnames (`"estes"`, `"freeman"`, `"fuller"`) stay excluded from `Tier_1_Gold` (Goodwin et al. 2015; Maldonado et al. 2015). Any non-type voucher carrying open-nomenclature qualifiers (`cf.`, `aff.`, `?`, `s.l.`) or two-epithet interspecific hybrid formulas (`is_open_or_hybrid_concept = 1`, while single-epithet named nothospecies like *Packera ×memmingeri* remain unflagged) is quarantined to `Tier_3_Bronze`.
  - **Layer 2 (`Tier_2_Silver` Regional/Historical Authorities, Heterotypic/Recency Caps, and Batch Demotion):** Regional flora authorities and historical *Senecioneae* workers (`REGIONAL_AND_HISTORICAL_SILVER_SURNAMES` paired with `REGIONAL_AND_HISTORICAL_SILVER_REGEXES`, including Cronquist, Fernald, Rydberg, Pippen, Turner, Löve, Greene, Gray, Small, Britton, and Campbell) route to `Tier_2_Silver` on non-type sheets and promote to `Tier_1_Gold` when verifying nomenclatural types. Investigator determinations (`J. Brandon Fuller` at qualified herbaria with rich locality), multi-herbarium corpus determiners, and curated herbaria likewise cap at `Tier_2_Silver`. In `apply_concept_and_segregate_recency_caps()`, I cap `Tier_1_Gold` down to `Tier_2_Silver` when a non-type determination rests on a `Heterotypic_Synonym` or precedes a sympatric post-1980 segregate protologue year inside its county or 50 km buffer (`Packera serpenticola` 2014 in Burke County NC; `Packera insulae-regalis` 2011 in Keweenaw County MI). Multi-herbarium monographer loans stay `Tier_1_Gold`, whereas undated single-herbarium single-species batch transfers ($\ge 25$ sheets across $\ge 10$ collectors with $\ge 85\%$ in one species where `recordedBy != identifiedBy`) demote to `Tier_2_Silver`.
* **Preflight Authority Refresh and Congeneric Neighbor Filtering (`run_pipeline.sh`):**
  During preflight, the orchestrator re-applies `apply_corpus_authority_tiers()` and filters `auto_neighbors` candidates against `^Packera\s+[a-z]{3,}$`, so `outgroup_taxa` always retains valid congeneric binomials and excludes malformed family tokens.

Now that Phase 1 establishes an authoritative, georeferenced specimen cohort, I feed these vetted vouchers directly into the vision pipeline.

## 3. Phase 2 Protocol: Pure LeafMachine3 ONNX Pipeline and Botanical Gatekeeping (`02_segment_and_extract.py`)

Herbarium sheets present challenging visual artifacts: white mounting tape and fragment packets alongside discolored paper and overlapping stems frequently cause standard bounding-box detectors to fail. I address this challenge by deploying **LeafMachine3** (`lm3`) ONNX Runtime models alongside botanical gatekeeper algorithms.

### 3.1 LeafMachine3 Inference Stack (`scripts/vision/`)
* **Scale Calibration (`RulerClassifierModel`-Gated Modules 1, 6, and 7):** Module 1 (`lm3_mp_conversion_factor__sqrt_fit`, $R^2 = 0.905$, MAE 2.3%) predicts resolution priors; Module 6 ensemble detects archival rulers via `lm3_ruler_classifier`; Module 7 (`lm3_ruler_segmenter` / `RulerCFLattice` paired with verified-crop deterministic 1D FFT and autocorrelation `solve_ruler_tick_lattice_px_per_mm` fallback alongside FieldPrism markers) establishes physical scale (`pixels_per_mm`, `is_calibrated_mm`). Inside `LM3ScaleCalibrator.calibrate_sheet()`, I gate `RulerCFLattice` predictions through `RulerClassifierModel` so explicitly imperial or inch rulers (`INCH`, `STD_IN`, `IMPERIAL` at confidence $\ge 0.45$) never calibrate as metric millimeters, while lattice estimates falling outside `[0.35, 2.25]` of the megapixel prior require verified-crop metric tick corroboration before `is_calibrated_mm` turns true. Unboxed full-sheet outer-margin tick detections remain tagged `tick_lattice_unboxed` (`is_calibrated_mm = False`).
* **Stage 1 Plant Detection, Dissected PCD Settings and Asymmetric Nested-Box Deduplication (`lm3_plant_detector__yolo26x_det_1280`):** I ingest herbarium sheets into a `max_working_dim = 3200` working frame (`cv2.INTER_AREA`) and execute Stage 1 plant and organ detection at $1280 \times 1280$ resolution (`conf = 0.35`, `iou = 0.55`) with dynamic rectangular stride-32 letterboxing (`ultra_rep.letterbox(auto=True, stride=32)`, `cv2.INTER_LINEAR_EXACT`) alongside float32 reciprocal scaling (`*= float32(1.0) / float32(255.0)`). I apply parent-preserving asymmetric nested-box deduplication (`overlap = 0.95`), so when a smaller leaf box sits nested inside an enclosing parent leaf box ($\text{area}_{\text{parent}} \ge 1.35 \cdot \text{area}_{\text{child}}$ and $\text{intersection} / \text{area}_{\text{child}} \ge 0.90$), I retain the full-leaf parent box and suppress the nested sub-lobe box.
* **Whole-Sheet Specimen Segmentation and CIE $L^*a^*b^*$ Paper Cleaning (`lm3_specimen_segmenter`):** Module 4 (`Specimen_Segmenter` `UNet++ v9`, $768 \times 768$) runs on the wave coordinator thread (`precomputed_spec_mask`) so CPU post-processing workers never contend on GPU locks. It generates 4-class whole-sheet probability maps (`bg` and `plant_main` alongside `text_bars` and `paper`), and I apply `paperclean_llr_hyst_keepblobs` (`LLR_Hyst_KeepBlobs`) log-likelihood hysteresis at `max_working_dim = 3200` strictly at the whole-sheet level to isolate the herbarium mounting sheet (`specimen_paper_cleaned_mask`) and plant foreground (`plant_binary_mask`).
* **Stage 2 Uncut Lamina Segmentation, Pinna Retention, and Read-Only Void/Fold QC (`lm3_leaf_segmenter__yolo26x_seg_1024`):** I extract tight `crop_pad = 0.0` bounding-box crops from Stage 1 detections (`conf = 0.35`, with `Leaf_PARTIAL` fragments entering strictly as zero-whole-leaf rescues flagged `LEAF_PARTIAL_FRAGMENT`) at $1024 \times 1024$ resolution with dynamic rectangular stride-32 letterboxing (`auto=True, stride=32`), `retina_masks: true`, and `report.masks.subtract_holes: true`. Inside `_reduce_crop_predictions_native_lm3()` and `_unpack_reduced_lm3_crop_preds()`, I keep all interior non-border-touching connected components (`>= 64` px) of LM3's focal `Leaf` (`class_id == 0`) mask intact so dissected basal pinnae on taxa such as *Packera millefolium* and *Packera ×memmingeri* are never truncated, while border-clipped neighbor fragments stay disentangled. Saved binary masks (`data/masks/*.png`), 120-vertex contours (`data/contours/*.csv` and `contour_xy_json`), and $32 \times 360$ ECT arrays (`data/ect/*.npy`) remain 100% uncut native `LeafMachine3` upright `Leaf & ~Hole` masks intersected solely with LM3's own `specimen_paper_cleaned_mask` interior pockets. In `scripts/vision/step02_leaf_traits_utils.py`, `measure_enclosed_interlobe_void_frac()` measures enclosed non-hole background voids (`enclosed_void_frac > 0.020` flags `ENCLOSED_INTERLOBE_VOID`) strictly read-only without carving or drawing lines onto any mask. In `evaluate_leaf_damage_and_fold_qc()` and `evaluate_step03_lm3_efa_qc()`, I screen out border-truncated blades (`CROP_BORDER_CLIPPED`), severe insect holes (`hole_area_frac > 0.035`), bent or dog-eared midveins (`FOLDED_MIDVEIN_TRACE`, `APICAL_TIP_FOLD`), longitudinal half-folds (`LONGITUDINAL_MARGIN_CREASE`), and unilateral tears (`UNILATERAL_ASYMMETRY_OR_TEAR`), and I grant dissected Dice tolerance (`bilateral_dice >= 0.58`, `|si_a| < 0.28`) strictly when `bilateral_lobe_symmetry` verifies multi-lobe inflections on both margins.
* **31-Keypoint Pose, Obovate Polarity Reconciliation, and Read-Only *Packera* Leaf/Vestiture Traits (`lm3_landmark_detector__yolo26x_pose_640`):** I pad each leaf crop with a 10% white border (`WHITE_PAD_FRAC = 0.10`, RGB `[255, 255, 255]`) and apply dynamic rectangular stride-32 letterboxing at $640 \times 640$ resolution. I predict 31 anatomical keypoints (`mid15_pet5`) and normalize 1-based canonical keys (`midvein_1..15`, `petiole_1..5`, `petiole_6`) to distinct 0-based native `LeafMachine3` `KPT_NAMES` (`midvein_0..14`, `petiole_0..4`, `petiole_tip`) without key collisions. When `petiole_mask` contacts predicted `lamina_tip` (`<= max(8.0, 0.08 * span_bt)`) far from `lamina_base` (`> 0.35 * span_bt`) and `crown_hint_xy` or direct petiole attachment corroborates inversion, `orient_and_score_leaf_lm3()` swaps poles together with `apex_left`/`apex_right` and `base_left`/`base_right` arms up to `_kp_conf_min < 0.88` so obovate and spatulate leaves (*Packera obovata*, *Packera paupercula*, *Packera dubia*) stay apex-upward. I delegate petiole arc lengths and landmark geometry directly to `leafmachine3.core.petiole.measure_petiole` and `leafmachine3.core.landmark_metrics.compute_measurements`, which forwards `base_angle_deg`, `apex_angle_deg`, `lamina_curvature`, and `lamina_trace_length_px` alongside upright `blade_length_mm` (preserving cordate basal auricles). Read-only `extract_packera_leaf_margin_and_base_traits()` adds `basal_cordate_index`, `margin_serration_index`, `margin_tooth_acuteness`, and `terminal_lobe_area_frac`. For vestiture photometry, `compute_calibrated_blade_photometry()` applies narrow-segment-safe erosion (`0.22 * max_d`), masks a `keypoints_31` midrib corridor when $\ge 24$ lateral pixels remain, distinguishes fibrous snow-white lanate wool (`DoG_MAD_SD > 1.8`) from smooth rim-to-rim mounting straps (`DoG_MAD_SD <= 1.8`, `ar_rect >= 2.8`), and exports `blade_cie_l_q85_sheet_delta` alongside `basal_zone_cie_l_delta`. Within each `(catalogNumber, plant_individual_id)` rosette containing $\ge 3$ basal leaves, `evaluate_rosette_abaxial_leaf_flags()` isolates upturned white-abaxial leaves (`+15.0 L*` above sibling median at low chroma `<= 18.0`), copies their reflectance to `abaxial_cie_l_sheet_delta`, and nulls their adaxial `blade_cie_l_norm` and `blade_cie_l_sheet_delta` while leaving shape contours and ECTs active.
* **Radial-and-Angular Euler Characteristic Transform (ECT) and Product Rendering:** I delegate $32 \times 360$ (`ect_num_dirs = 360, num_thresh = 32`) radial-and-angular Euler Characteristic Transform computation and visual product rendering directly to `leafmachine3.core.ect_compute.compute_ect` and `leafmachine3.reporting.ect_products.ect_product_for` alongside `leafmachine3.reporting.leaf_products.render_leaf_products` (`want_simple=False` in `_render_lm3_ect_rgb()` so visual badges match the analytical `32 x 360` ECT arrays saved to `data/ect/*_ect.npy` and indexed by `ect_path`).

### 3.2 Read-Only LeafMachine3 Ingestion, Organ Indexing and Scale Calibration (`scripts/pipeline/02_segment_and_extract.py`)
* **2D Root-Anchored DBSCAN Plant Partitioning (`cluster_plant_individuals`):** I inspect `root_boxes` before whole-sheet `specimen_mask` unioning so sheets bearing two or more roots separated by $> \max(45\text{ mm}, 0.16 \cdot \max(H, W))$ partition touching `specimen_mask` bridges by 2D root-crown proximity and veto vertical shoot merges across distinct root owners (`plant_individual_id = 0, 1, ...`).
* **Rosette Caudex Crown Estimation and Direct Petiole/Scape Metrics (`estimate_rosette_crown_point`, `approximate_petiole_and_scape_metrics`):** I localize the root-shoot transition zone ($p_{\text{crown}}$) from root boxes and inward basal midrib rays. I evaluate scape height ($H_{\text{plant}}$) across straight and bent stems:
  $$H_{\text{plant}} = \|p_{\text{bend}} - p_{\text{crown}}\|_2 + \|p_{\text{corymb}} - p_{\text{bend}}\|_2$$
* **Lateral-Only Capitulum Geometry and Optical Phenology (`compute_capitulum_metrics`, `capitulum_phenology_classifier.py`):** On flowering and fruiting heads (`class_id in (2, 4)`, never `6` Bud), I exclude distal oxidized amber/ochre corollas from `involucre_cand` and flag top-down circular radial discs as `is_lateral_profile = False` with `NULL` involucre dimensions so head counts increment without corrupting lateral `AR_inv`. * **Topological and Heteroblastic Anatomical `leaf_zone` Classification (`classify_leaf_zone_anatomical`) and Read-Only CPU Re-Scoring (`rescore_step02_qc_and_zones.py`):** Because acropetal heteroblasty between petiolate basal rosette leaves and subpetiolate or sessile cauline stem leaves drives morphological contrast across North American *Packera* (Barkley 1962; Trock 2006), `classify_leaf_zone_anatomical()` in `scripts/vision/organ_crown_metrics.py` resolves shoot zones polarity-invariantly across four organ-presence regimes: (1) `Roots` and `Capitula` jointly present, (2) `Roots` present alone (with proximal petiolate leaves anchoring a caudex box when bare roots are sparse), (3) `Capitula`-only flowering stalks applying the `Case 3B` clipped aerial stem veto (`raw_d_crown > 1.35 * r_hub` when stem fraction `s_prelim > 0.28`, or `> 0.18` near capitula), and (4) organless `Case 4A`/`4B` rosette mounts. Long-petiole flowering rosette leaves (`petiole_to_blade_ratio >= 0.35` and distal stem fraction `s_stem_tip <= 0.32`) rescue intact basal leaves near the root crown, whereas sessile narrow blades along elevated aerial nodes override cleanly to `cauline`. The classifier outputs continuous basal probabilities `p_basal` (`leaf_zone = "basal"` when `p_basal >= 0.50`, else `"cauline"`) alongside `leaf_zone_confidence = round(max(p_basal, 1.0 - p_basal), 4)` and `dist_from_crown_norm`. To recalibrate leaf zones, read-only void QC (`measure_enclosed_interlobe_void_frac`), *Packera* margin/base traits, `bbox_x1..bbox_y2`-backed upright photometry (`--recompute-photometry`), abaxial rosette flags, and margin tick-lattice ruler scales across already segmented sheets without mutating masks or `contour_xy_json`, I run `scripts/vision/rescore_step02_qc_and_zones.py` on CPU.
* **Read-Only LeafMachine3 Persistence and Downstream Step 03 QC (`evaluate_step03_lm3_efa_qc`):** Step 02 operates as a strictly read-only `LeafMachine3` ingestion adapter that persists 100% of uncut segmented leaf instances, sheet boxes (`bbox_x1..bbox_y2`), native Voronoi bilateral symmetry metrics (`bilateral_dice`, `si_a`), void-aware QC indicators (`efa_qc_pass`, `qc_flag_reason`), 120-vertex tip-up `lamina_base`-anchored contours (`contour_xy_json`), and $32 \times 360$ ECT arrays (`data/ect/*.npy`) directly to SQLite `extracted_leaves`. * **300-DPI PNG Organ Montages and Native Visual Caches (`scripts/vision/step02_figures.py`):** Step 02 exports downstream-QC-filtered 300-DPI PNG plates (`fig02a_lm3_organ_and_ect_exemplars.png` and `fig02b_segmentation_qc_and_phenophase.png`), unlimited taxon-indexed `LeafMachine3` RGB cutouts (hydrated from `bbox_x1..bbox_y2` and rotated to match upright masks) alongside analytical `want_simple=False` polar ECT badges (`lm3_assets/` and `ect_overlays/`), and capped whole-sheet diagnostic overlays (`lm3_sheet_overlays/`, `max_sheets = 24`).

By partitioning basal rosette and cauline stem leaves into explicit anatomical zones while I reject severed fragments and mounting tape artifacts, I preserve developmental homology across both foliar series.

## 4. Phase 3 and 4 Protocol: Morphometrics, EFA, Topological ECT, Allometry and Ordination (`03` and `04`)

In Phase 3 and Phase 4, I transform discrete leaf outlines into continuous mathematical and topological functions. At first glance, elliptic Fourier analysis and Euler Characteristic Transform topology seem different, but that difference is superficial because both approaches quantify boundary and sinus variance relative to biological homology.

### 4.1 Homologized Closed-Contour EFA, OTU-Anchored $32 \times 360$ ECT Decomposition and 3-Way Confidence Routing (`03_fourier_extractor.R`, `step03_config_scale_utils.R`, `step03_ect_traits_utils.R`, `dissected_leaf_utils.R`, and `fourier_parallel_utils.R`)
* **Closed-Contour QC Gating (`evaluate_step03_lm3_efa_qc`), Shape-Free Zone Routing & `<= 35 mm` Proximity Consolidation (`classify_and_partition_leaf_zones`, `consolidate_fragmented_sheet_individuals`, `apply_otu_confidence_routing_tier`):**
  I restrict `Momocs::efourier` strictly to intact closed contours passing `evaluate_step03_lm3_efa_qc()` and `efa_qc_pass == 1`, so I withhold damaged, border-clipped, folded, and unresolved clump masks while retaining hole-preserved $32 \times 360$ ECT arrays for deeply incised multi-component blades. Next, `classify_and_partition_leaf_zones()` partitions passing leaves across three confidence-gated shoot zones strictly from crown distance (`dist_from_crown_norm`) and plant-box vertical position (`cy_rel_plant_bbox` / `cy_rel_sheet`) with zero `petiole_to_blade_ratio` or blade-area circularity: `basal` (`leaf_zone == "basal"` with `leaf_zone_confidence >= 0.65`), `ambiguous_transitional` (putative basal blades below the `0.65` confidence gate), and `cauline` (for the orthogonal stem-leaf and heteroblasty track, where the function rescues lower-confidence blades only when no `>= 0.65` basal leaf survives and remaining leaves are not all crown-verified cauline). Before OTU aggregation, `consolidate_fragmented_sheet_individuals()` derives missing `cx_px`/`cy_px` centroids from `bbox_x1..bbox_y2` and merges isolated cauline or single-leaf fragments onto the primary basal rosette only when physical distance stays within `<= 35.0 mm` (`<= 0.12` normalized sheet span when uncalibrated). Whenever all pixel and relative sheet coordinates are missing, the function keeps distinct `plant_individual_id` detections separate. During individual OTU assembly, `apply_otu_confidence_routing_tier()` sets `morph_tier_source = "Transitional_Passive"` when an individual relies on `< 0.65` transitional leaves and `"Cauline_Only_Fallback"` when zero basal blades remain. Both `Transitional_Passive` and `Cauline_Only_Fallback` individuals receive `is_passive = TRUE` and `authority_weight = 0.0` (unless pinned as a verified nomenclatural type), so Step 03 and Step 04 strictly isolate transitional lower-stem blades and cauline fallback sheets from primary `Basal_Rosette` reference covariance matrices, `residualize_ontogenetic_phenophase_plasticity()`, and `audit_institutional_batch_effects()`. All individual `basal` and `cauline` leaf vectors plus zone classifications persist to `leaf_efa_harmonics` alongside `leaf_efa_harmonics_individual.csv`.
* **Scale Provenance Gating (`resolve_step03_calibration_provenance`) & Step 02 Trait / Photometry Handoff (`aggregate_step02_traits_by_otu`):**
  `resolve_step03_calibration_provenance()` inside `step03_config_scale_utils.R` alongside `fetch_step03_contours_from_db()` in `db_utils.R` marks `mp_estimated`, `predicted_from_megapixels`, `tick_lattice_unboxed`, and `uncalibrated` leaves as `is_calibrated_mm = FALSE`, preserves `tick_lattice_unboxed` verbatim in `scale_source`, and sets `centroid_size`, `log_centsize`, `area_mm2`, `petiole_length_mm`, `petiole_width_mm`, `blade_length_mm`, `blade_width_mm`, `lamina_trace_length_mm`, and `cauline_blade_length_mm` to `NA` on both individual and OTU outputs while preserving dimensionless `petiole_to_blade_ratio`. `fetch_step03_contours_from_db()` resolves `mask_is_upright` individually per leaf from each contour's `lm3_from_tipup` attribute, and it hydrates all 16 Step 02 leaf-architecture and photometry columns alongside `bbox_x1..bbox_y2` plus Step 01 taxonomic concept and quarantine metadata. Inside `step03_ect_traits_utils.R`, `aggregate_step02_traits_by_otu()` computes primary-zone OTU medians across all seven `STEP03_LEAF_ARCH_MED_COLS` (`base_angle_deg`, `apex_angle_deg`, `lamina_curvature`, `basal_cordate_index`, `margin_serration_index`, `margin_tooth_acuteness`, `terminal_lobe_area_frac`) alongside ruler-gated Q75 `lamina_trace_length_mm` (`type = 7`). The helper separates adaxial lamina photometry (`blade_cie_l_norm`, `blade_cie_l_sd`, `blade_cie_l_sheet_delta`, `blade_cie_l_q85_sheet_delta`, `blade_cie_a_delta`, `blade_cie_b_delta` computed strictly over `!is_abaxial_pressed_leaf` primary blades whenever at least one finite adaxial leaf exists) from upturned white-tomentose abaxial rosettes (`is_abaxial_pressed_leaf`, `abaxial_leaf_fraction`, `abaxial_cie_l_sheet_delta`), whereas `basal_zone_cie_l_delta` aggregates across all primary leaves.
* **Vertex-1-Preserving Tip-Up Homology, Outline Reconstruction and Harmonic Power Calibration:**
  Across both SQLite and CSV ingestion, I orient contours clockwise via `ensure_clockwise_keep_start_r()` so row 1 (`Point 1`, homologous `lamina_base`) always stays anchored at index 1 without cyclic shifting. I then center and isotropically scale `lamina_base -> lamina_tip` homologized contours via `Momocs::coo_center() %>% Momocs::coo_scale()` paired with horizontal major-axis alignment (`align_contour_horizontal_xax()`, which enforces `dx[1] <= 0` so `Point 1` maps to `-X` and `Point 61` maps to `+X` across all three alignment branches), which preserves true length-to-width aspect ratios below $1.0$ on reniform and cordate blades. For visual morphospace deformation grids, `reconstruct_efa_outline()` in `pub_figures_utils.R` orients every reconstructed blade directly from homologous `t = 0` (`lamina_base` at `-Y` and `lamina_tip` at `+Y`) so wider-than-long cordate and reniform outlines render upright without aspect-ratio flipping. Harmonic power is defined via `load_step03_fourier_config()` YAML/CLI bounds across all harmonics $n = 1..H_{\max}$ (including $n = 1$ in `fig03a` Panel A) as:
  $$\text{Power}_n = \frac{A_n^2 + B_n^2 + C_n^2 + D_n^2}{2},\quad \frac{\sum_{n=1}^{H_{\text{opt}}} \text{Power}_n}{\sum_{n=1}^{H_{\max}} \text{Power}_n} \ge 0.99$$
  I dynamically determine the harmonic count $H_{\text{opt}} \in [6, 24]$ that captures $\ge 99\%$ cumulative power, and I execute `Momocs::efourier(out_obj, nb.h = H_opt, norm = FALSE, start = FALSE)`.
* **Leaf-Level Antisymmetry Retention vs. OTU Bilateral Symmetrization (Claude 2008):**
  $$A_n^{\text{sym}} = A_n,\quad D_n^{\text{sym}} = D_n,\quad B_n^{\text{sym}} = 0,\quad C_n^{\text{sym}} = 0,\quad \text{pct\_asymmetry} = \frac{\sum_{n=1}^{H_{\text{opt}}} (B_n^2 + C_n^2)}{\sum_{n=1}^{H_{\text{opt}}} (A_n^2 + B_n^2 + C_n^2 + D_n^2)}$$
  Individual leaf records in `leaf_efa_harmonics` and `leaf_efa_harmonics_individual.csv` retain both symmetric `A1..A{H}, D1..D{H}` and raw leaf-level antisymmetric `B1..B{H}, C1..C{H}` harmonics (all masked to `NA` / SQL `NULL` on `non_jordan_mask` void rows where `enclosed_void_frac > 0.020`), whereas specimen OTU records in `otu_morphometrics` and `leaf_efa_harmonics.csv` persist strictly symmetric `A1..A{H}, D1..D{H}` harmonics whenever `symmetrize_efa_harmonics == TRUE`.
* **OTU-Anchored Topological Euler Characteristic Transform (ECT) Alignment, Symmetrization and SVD (`extract_step03_leaf_ect_vectors`, `fit_and_project_otu_ect_pca`):**
  Because Fourier harmonics can smooth deep pinnatisect sinuses and fail on multi-island compound blades, `extract_step03_leaf_ect_vectors()` in `scripts/morphometrics/step03_ect_traits_utils.R` loads each leaf's $32 \times 360$ radial-and-angular ECT matrix from `data/ect/*_ect.npy` (or computes the exact EmbeddedGraph cycle Euler characteristic $\chi(\phi_j, t_i) = \#V(h \le t_i) - \#E(\max(h_u, h_v) \le t_i)$ across 360 angular directions and 32 radial thresholds via `compute_cycle_ect_r()`; Ayub et al. 2026; Weaver 2026). Column 1 is anchored to the leaf apex ($\phi = 0$), and directional columns $2..\text{nc}$ are reflected to form the bilateral symmetric half-matrix $\mathbf{E}_{\text{sym}} = \frac{1}{2}(\mathbf{E} + \mathbf{E}_{\text{refl}})$ under a signed log-modulus transform $\operatorname{sign}(\mathbf{E}_{\text{sym}})\log(1 + |\mathbf{E}_{\text{sym}}|)$. From each matrix, I compute bilateral ECT asymmetry (`ect_bilateral_asym`) and topological lobe complexity (`ect_lobe_complexity` and `cauline_ect_lobe_complexity`) as the sum of excess directional total variation $\operatorname{mean}_j(\max(0, (\text{TV}_j - 2)/2))$ and multi-component sinus excess $\operatorname{mean}(\max(0, \chi - 1))$. To prevent multi-leaf rosettes from dominating topological ordination, `fit_and_project_otu_ect_pca()` aggregates valid primary-zone leaf ECT vectors to 1 coordinate-wise median vector per `(catalogNumber, plant_individual_id)` OTU, fits authority- and spatial-weighted SVD (`ECT_PC1..PC3`) strictly on active `Basal_Rosette` anchors (`Tier_3_Bronze` weight `0`, while admitting multi-island `non_jordan_mask` basal anchors with valid ECT), sign-locks `ECT_PC1` positively to `ect_lobe_complexity`, and projects both OTUs and individual leaves onto the shared `(mu, V_3)` basis alongside sign-locked orthogonal cauline tracks (`Cauline_PC1` locked to `cauline_dissection_index_med`; `Cauline_ECT_PC1` locked to `cauline_ect_lobe_complexity`) and within-plant basal-to-cauline topological divergence (`heteroblasty_ect_distance`).
* **Type Outlier Immunity, Sign-Locked Post-MCD OTU PCA, Pure Basal Primary OTU Aggregation and Orthogonal Cauline Track:**
  I exempt intact type contours from within-taxon Mahalanobis and MCD outlier screening (`is_contour_outlier[is_type_specimen] <- FALSE`). To eliminate multi-leaf pseudoreplication in ordination space, Step 03 fits one authoritative weighted PCA (`stats::cov.wt()`) strictly at the `Basal_Rosette` OTU level across active anchors (`authority_weight * spatial_balance_weight`, with `PC1` sign-locked positively to `-A1`), propagates OTU authority status onto leaves (`0.0` passive weight for non-basal or outlier non-type leaves), projects both OTUs and leaves into that shared space, and screens leaf PCs via `screen_regime_stratified_mcd_outliers()`. Whenever a multi-leaf rosette sheds an outlier leaf, Step 03 re-aggregates OTU medians and re-fits both `.fit_weighted_morphospace_pca()` and `fit_and_project_otu_ect_pca()` on the clean post-MCD OTUs so exported `PC1..PC{k_export}` and `ECT_PC1..PC3` stay exact (`k_export = max(5, k_95)`, bounded by YAML `min_export_pcs` and `max_export_pcs`). During coordinate-wise OTU aggregation to `(catalogNumber, plant_individual_id)` via `aggregate_step03_otu_medians_fast()` (`reconcile_step03_basal_otu_medians()` delegates to the same helper), I compute unweighted primary OTU medians strictly across clean `leaf_zone == 'basal'` leaves whenever $\ge 1$ basal leaf is present on the specimen (falling back to cauline medians as `Cauline_Only_Fallback` only when a specimen has 0 basal leaves), so cauline bracts never contaminate the basal rosette outline. Simultaneously, I aggregate `leaf_zone == 'cauline'` leaves into an orthogonal cauline and heteroblasty track (`cauline_leaf_count`, `cauline_solidity_med`, `cauline_dissection_index_med`, `cauline_aspect_ratio_med`, `cauline_blade_length_mm` on calibrated rows, `cauline_ect_lobe_complexity`, `basal_vs_cauline_length_ratio`, `basal_vs_cauline_solidity_delta`, `heteroblasty_efa_distance`, `heteroblasty_ect_distance`, `Cauline_PC1..PC3`, and `Cauline_ECT_PC1..PC2`), and `step03_figures.R` marks nomenclatural types with matching double-ringed hollow diamonds (`shape = 5`) on both `fig03a` Panel B and `fig03b` Panel A.

### 4.2 Determiner Authority Weighting, Spatial Population Demes and Reference Anchors (`authority_weighting_utils.R`, `plasticity_mitigation_utils.R`)
* **Hierarchical Heteroblasty Partitioning, 10 km Spatial Demes and Plasticity Mitigation:**
  To account for within-plant heteroblasty and local microsite plasticity without pre-shrinking feature coordinates before cross-validation or MANOVA, I separate variance sources across three explicit stages:
  - **Level 1 (Orthogonal Basal and Cauline Heteroblasty Partitioning):** Primary OTU coordinates represent unweighted basal rosette medians (`aggregate_step03_otu_medians_fast()`), whereas acropetal leaf dimorphism is modeled simultaneously through the orthogonal cauline and heteroblasty feature track (`Cauline_PC1..PC3`, `Cauline_ECT_PC1..PC2`, `basal_vs_cauline_solidity_delta`, `heteroblasty_ect_distance`).
  - **Level 2 (10 km Conspecific Spatial Deme Assignment):** In `assign_spatial_gathering_demes()`, conspecific individuals within a $10\text{ km}$ single-linkage Haversine radius (and shared gathering keys) receive deterministic `population_deme_id` and `deme_n_specimens` identifiers while preserving raw specimen morphometric coordinates for independent LOOCV and MANOVA hypothesis testing.
  - **Level 3 (Homogeneous-Slope Anthesis-Referenced Phenophase Adjustment, Centroid-Quarantined Photometry Batch Centering and Hennig Ridgeline Merging):** In `scripts/morphometrics/plasticity_mitigation_utils.R`, `merge_voucher_phenophase_covariates()` joins optical capitulum counts from `voucher_phenological_states.csv` or directly from SQLite `voucher_reproductive_phenology` when the CSV is absent (`n_anthesis`, `n_bud`, `n_fruiting`, `n_senescent`, `total_capitula`, `corymb_head_count_est`, `radiate_head_fraction`). Next, `residualize_ontogenetic_phenophase_plasticity()` leaves size allometry strictly to Step 04 `test_and_correct_allometry()` and derives `fruiting_frac` using `pmax(tot_cap, corymb_head_count_est, na.rm = TRUE)`. It protects reproductive head and involucre traits (`AR_inv`, `log1p_capitulum_count`, `radiate_head_fraction`, and their `*_std` fields), requires homogeneous taxon-by-fruiting slopes (`p_int > 0.05` on `y ~ fruiting_frac * sub_sp`) across active pure-basal vouchers (`morph_tier_source == "Basal_Rosette"`), and adjusts passing traits strictly to the `fruiting_frac = 0.0` anthesis baseline (`y - b_fr * fruiting_frac`). Downstream, `apply_eb_institution_batch_centering()` excludes `is_centroid_or_inst_coord == TRUE` sheets when evaluating regional herbarium confounding while logging `pre_eb_batch_partial_eta_sq` diagnostics across traits. It restricts shrinkage centering to technical photometry columns (`^(blade_cie_|basal_zone_cie_l_delta|abaxial_cie_l_sheet_delta|blade_.*browning)`) only when `allow_shape_centering = FALSE` alongside Cramer's $V < 0.45$ and dominant regional share $< 0.75$; finally, `merge_plastic_gmm_components_ridgeline()` merges overlapping Gaussian mixture modes via Hennig ridgeline density connectivity (`ridgeline_ratio_thresh`) prior to Step 07 lineage gating.
* **Two-Stage Gold/Silver Anchor Seeding, Homotypic vs. Heterotypic Type Control and Confident Learning Pruning:**
  In `select_adaptive_reference_anchors()`, I exclude non-type open-nomenclature and two-epithet hybrid rows (`is_open_or_hybrid_concept`) alongside extralimital records from `anchor_pool`, stamp them as `"Passive_Open_or_Hybrid_Concept"`, and assign `authority_weight = 0.0` in `compute_empirical_bayes_authority_weights()` across all weighting modes. When $\ge 3$ non-heterotypic `Tier_1_Gold` or Type anchors exist for a taxon, reference medoid estimation prefers `!is_het_syn_non_type` rows, while type shrinkage anchors strictly on homotypic *sensu stricto* name-bearing types (`gs_nb_type_local & gs_homo_type_local`) whenever available so divergent heterotypic types never pull the species medoid. Taxa with $\ge \max(5, p_{\text{eff}} + 3)$ `Tier_1_Gold` + `Tier_0_Type` anchors seed the initial FastMCD reference ellipsoid on Gold and Type specimens ($\chi^2_{0.95, p}$; minimum sample size $n \ge \max(12, 5p)$ per Rousseeuw & Van Driessen 1999) and screen candidate `Tier_2_Silver` vouchers against that envelope at $\chi^2_{0.90, p}$ before refitting the combined anchor covariance. When a taxon exhibits bimodal basal leaf dissection across its `Tier_1_Gold` anchors ($\ge 2$ dissected and $\ge 2$ entire Gold vouchers with basal solidity span $> 0.15$), sub-mode FastMCD screening evaluates whole and dissected morphotypes jointly without static species lists. Active anchors are screened via leave-one-out shrinkage discriminant posteriors $\hat{P}_{-i}(y = k \mid x_i)$ with per-class self-confidence thresholds $t_j$ (Northcutt et al. 2021). Anchors that violate their assigned class threshold while exceeding another class's threshold are flagged (`is_confident_label_noise = TRUE`), demoted to `"Passive_Confident_Noise_Demoted"`, and projected passively. Intact homotypic name-bearing types keep unconditional MCD retention immunity (`anchor_tier_source = "Tier_0_Type_Anchor"`), whereas extreme heterotypic types exceeding $\chi^2_{0.995, p}$ when $\ge 5$ clean non-heterotypic Gold or homotypic type anchors exist demote into `"Passive_Heterotypic_Type_Audit"` (`active_mask = FALSE`, `authority_weight = 0.0`). In `run_canonical_discriminant_analysis()`, I recompute `authority_weight` after `is_active` finalizes so passive and MCD-pruned rows alongside heterotypic-demoted types plus open/hybrid and confident label-noise rows always carry `authority_weight == 0`. Taxon reference centers shrink toward the homotypic type medoid:
  $$\boldsymbol{\mu}_{\text{ref}, k} \leftarrow (1 - \gamma_k) \boldsymbol{\mu}_{\text{ref}, k} + \gamma_k \boldsymbol{\mu}_{\text{type}, k},\quad \gamma_k = \min\!\left(0.35,\, \frac{2.5\,n_{\text{eff, type}, k}}{N_{\text{gs}, k} + 2.5\,n_{\text{eff, type}, k}}\right)$$
  Under `min_anchors_per_taxon = 5L`, the adaptive promotion target scales proportionally with the taxon pool (`eff_min_anc = min(5, floor(0.5 * n_tx_pool))` when `n_gs >= 2` and `n_tx_pool >= 5`, `n_gs` when `n_gs >= 2` in tiny fixtures, `min(2, n_tx_pool)` when `n_gs == 1`, and `min(5, n_tx_pool)` when `n_gs == 0`), promoting central `Tier_3_Bronze` vouchers within $\chi^2_{0.80}$ (`is_promoted_bronze = TRUE`, `anchor_tier_source = "Promoted_Bronze"`).
* **Empirical Bayes Determiner Authority Weights (Institution-Invariant):**

* **Smooth Continuous Habitat Predictors (`compute_derived_habitat_predictors`):**
  To expose ecologically interpretable gradients with identical scales across vouchers and SDM projection rasters without discontinuous jumps, I derive five mechanistic indices:
  1. `lithic_outcrop_index` $\in [0, 1]$: Maps continuous lithic depth (`fine_bedrock_depth_cm` from `anylithicdpt`) via $\text{bd\_score} = \operatorname{clamp}_{[0,1]}(1 - \ln(1 + \max(\text{bd\_cm}, 0)) / \ln(201))$ and blends `0.80 * bd_score + 0.20 * (log1p(max(cfvo_pct, 0)) / log1p(100))` when coarse rock fragments (`fine_cfvo_pct` or raster `cfvo`) are finite (`round(bd_score, 4)` otherwise); the index stays strictly invariant to `ssurgo_rock_outcrop_pct` and binary outcrop flags across both point rows and regional SDM stacks.
  2. `summer_moisture_deficit_idx`: Ratio of warmest-quarter thermal load (`bio10`) to driest-period precipitation (`bio17 / 3` or `bio14`), which separates summer-xeric western prairies and glades from summer-humid eastern woodlands.
  3. `maritime_continentality_idx`: Scales winter cold buffering (`bio6 + 20.0`) inversely against temperature seasonality (`bio4`) and `bio2_mean_diurnal_range`, which distinguishes coastal plain thermal regimes from continental interior basins.

* **Morphometric-Independent Niche Regime Discovery (`resolve_analysis_group` in `step05_phenology_ecotypes.R`):**

  Because polymorphic-only SNP matrices bias branch-length ratios under small sample sizes ($n = 1$ to $2$), I parse full concatenated `PHYLIP`/`NEXUS` alignments that retain invariant sites and IUPAC heterozygote codes across $122\text{ bp}$ locus blocks. Inside `step06_coalescent_models.py`, `select_nucleotide_substitution_model()` encodes IUPAC heterozygotes (`R`, `Y`, `M`, `K`, `S`, `W`, `B`, `D`, `H`, `V`) as exact 4-state partial-likelihood masks with evenly weighted empirical base frequencies, counts heterozygous IUPAC sites as variable when checking Lewis (2001) `+ASC` (`prop_invariant < 0.05` and `n_sites >= 30`), and evaluates `JC69`, `K80`, `F81`, `HKY85`, and `TN93` with and without `+G4`.   Values $\widehat{gdi} < 0.20$ diagnose a single oversplit lineage, whereas $\widehat{gdi} > 0.70$ with $Q_{12} < 0.15$ supports distinct species-level coalescence. To test secondary contact vs. pure ILS directly across blocks, `compute_coalescent_introgression_bf()` compares a single-rate coalescent null ($M_0$) against a two-component introgression mixture ($M_1$) via `bpp_stepping_stone` or `bic_coalescent_mixture` (`bf_10`, `w_introgr`, `tau_introgr`, `tau_sp`). ## 7. Phase 7 Protocol: Reliability-Weighted Consensus and Hierarchical Nomenclatural Type Decision Matrix (`07_triage_dashboard_synthesis.R`)

* **Master View Synthesis, Marginal Institutional ANCOVA, Weighted Hopkins Regression & Modality Reliability Weighting (`step07_ancova_merge_utils.R`, `step07_hopkins_consensus_utils.R`, `step07_consensus_weights_utils.R`, `compute_modality_reliability_weights`, `compute_probabilistic_consensus_scores`):**

### Four-Tier Unsequenced Voucher Genomic Propagation and Abstention Protocol (`step07_genomic_propagation.R`, `step07_deme_propagation_utils.R`)

Herbarium collections exhibit high misidentification rates across institutions (Goodwin et al. 2015), whereas reduced-representation sequencing covers only a fraction of mounted sheets. When sequenced `G1` or `G2` accessions are absent from the cohort, Step 07 leaves baseline consensus scores alongside authority weights and treatments strictly unchanged (`genomic_propagation_tier = NA`). When sequenced vouchers are present, `load_step07_genomic_metrics()` ranks multi-replicate libraries per catalog number cleanly (`G1_Direct_Voucher` > `G1_Exsiccatae_Isoduplicate` > `G2_Population_Voucher_Proxy`, then descending `geno_posterior_prob` and `hq_reads`), and I bridge genomic certainty onto unsequenced specimens strictly through four gated tiers in `step07_genomic_propagation.R` paired with `step07_deme_propagation_utils.R` so sympatric contact zones avoid blind label copying.

* **Tier 1 (`G1_Exsiccatae_Isoduplicate`) and Mixed-Gathering Mahalanobis Veto:**
  When a `G1_Direct_Voucher` or `G1_Exsiccatae_Isoduplicate` sample sequences one duplicate of an exsiccatae gathering sharing `exsiccatae_key`, Step 06 (`_link_sample_to_voucher()`) resolves duplicate key hits toward the primary imaged sheet (`is_primary_duplicate == TRUE`), and I propagate that genomic identity across unsequenced duplicates under `G1_Exsiccatae_Isoduplicate` with a `0.95` link weight scale (Nicolson et al. 2018). Because historical collectors occasionally mounted heterogeneous taxa under one gathering number, blind duplicate propagation risks spreading mixed-mount errors across herbaria (Goodwin et al. 2015). I therefore evaluate shared-subspace `Basal_Rosette` Mahalanobis distance (`PC1..6` union `ECT_PC1..3`, which covers dissected multi-island `ECT_PC1..3`-only rosettes whenever `>= 2` finite morphometric axes exist) against the sequenced duplicate (`deme_mahal_d2`), and I immediately veto propagation whenever `deme_mahal_d2 > qchisq(0.99, p)`, when an unsequenced sheet carries `is_type_specimen == TRUE`, or when a multi-plant sheet carries a morphometric outlier flag (`is_multi && is_mo`).
* **Tier 2 (`G2_Conditional_Deme_Hull`) Conflict-Only Gates, Soft Attenuation, and Micro-Sympatry Guard:**
  Neighboring rosettes from a single homogeneous outcrop often belong to one breeding deme, so I propagate local deme identity (`G2_Conditional_Deme_Hull`, weighted at baseline `0.75` and persisted across `[0.45, 0.75]` via `geno_deme_weight_scale` without unilateral Stage 1 misidentification override) whenever an unsequenced `Basal_Rosette` voucher clears four conflict-only gates. First, the specimen must lie within `<= 2.0 km` of a QC-passing sequenced `G1_Direct_Voucher`, `G1_Exsiccatae_Isoduplicate`, `G2`, or georeferenced `G3` anchor with `coordinateUncertaintyInMeters <= 2000 m` or missing precision (`NA`), and it receives a `0.85x` soft weight factor whenever precision lies in `(1000, 2000] m` or an `NA`-precision voucher sits at `(1.0, 2.0] km`. Second, `is_azonal_lithology` must match the anchor when both values are finite, and soil chemistry passes cleanly at `type_edaphic_z_score <= 1.75` or local anchor `fine_soil_ph` agreement (`<= 0.60`), takes a `0.88x` attenuation factor across `(1.75, 2.50]` or up to `3.0` with local pH difference `<= 1.00`, and vetoes beyond those bounds. Third, the voucher must lie inside the 95% shared-subspace `Basal_Rosette` Mahalanobis hull (`deme_mahal_d2 <= qchisq(0.95, p)`, which covers `ECT_PC1..3`-only sheets via `.compute_morph_pool_prop()`) while flowering within `tau_deme_doy` (clipped to `[24, 28]` days, default `26.0`) of the local anchor's active-anthesis `hopkins_adjusted_doy` or taxon mean, with `0.88x` attenuation across `(18, tau_deme_doy]` days. Fourth, my micro-sympatry guard modeled on mixed sites such as Buck Creek and `VAL1` evaluates only verified anchors (`is_type_specimen` and `Tier_0` DNA anchors alongside non-outlier `Tier_1_Gold` vouchers) within `<= 5.0 km` for genomic anchors or `<= 2.0 km` for cohort anchors and aborts when $D^2_{\text{alt}} - \text{deme\_mahal\_d2} < 4.0$, so unsequenced `Tier_2_Silver` or `Tier_3_Bronze` misidentifications never poison pure sites. Across multi-cytotype taxa, `G2` transfer vetoes strictly on observed cytotype conflicts inside the local `2.0 km` deme rather than missing ploidy or distant allopatric populations, while applying a `0.85x` soft discount whenever recipient or local deme ploidy is unobserved.
* **Tier 3 (`Tier_0` Weight `4.0` Anchor Calibration, Contradicted Anchor Eviction, and Four-Condition Abstention):**
  DNA-verified accessions establish rigorous calibration standards for morphological discrimination when historical determinations disagree (Marhold et al. 2010; Giaco et al. 2022; Rocha et al. 2023). I assign verified `G1` sequenced anchors `Tier_0` weight (`4.0`) for reference centroids, circular phenology, and discriminant training while quarantining passive heterotypic or uncertain-coordinate types alongside open/hybrid concepts (`Passive_Heterotypic_Type_Audit`, `Passive_Type_Uncertain_Coord`, `Passive_Open_or_Hybrid_Concept`, `concept_synonymy_status == "Heterotypic_Synonym"`, or `is_open_or_hybrid_concept = 1`) from `.build_taxon_centroids()` and `resolve_tier0_anchor_overrides()` (granting type override immunity strictly to clean homotypic types): whenever a `Tier_1_Gold` or lower voucher is contradicted by its own `G1` sequence at posterior probability `>= 0.85` (or displays admixed `interspecific_ancestry_q12 >= 0.35`), `resolve_tier0_anchor_overrides()` sets `evict_from_given_anchor = TRUE` so Step 07 (`07_triage_dashboard_synthesis.R`) removes that specimen from reference morphology and phenology pools for its nominal `given_sp`. Evaluating leave-one-population-out cross-validation (`LOPO-CV`) on a temperature-scaled Ledoit-Wolf shrinkage discriminant classifier (`lambda = 0.20`, which accepts rows with `>= 3` finite morphometric axes or complete `ECT_PC` tracks) anchored on vetted `Tier_0` specimens, I populate `tier0_calibrated_taxon` and `tier0_calibrated_prob` (`tier0_abstention_flag = 0`) on well-separated unsequenced specimens and inject a soft consensus prior (`w_t0 = 0.50 * lopo_cv_kappa`) only when `geno_link_scale == 0.0`, `tier0_abstention_flag == 0`, and `tier0_calibrated_prob >= 0.80`. Conversely, abstention triggers set `tier0_abstention_flag = 1`, record `tier0_calibrated_taxon = "Sensu_Lato_DNA_Required"`, block Stage 3 and Stage 4 splitting calls, and append `[Tier_0 Abstention: Phenotypic overlap across genetically distinct lineages; DNA required]` to `synthesis_rationale` whenever `p1 < 0.70`, top-two posterior margin `p1 - p2 < 0.15`, `LOPO-CV` pairwise error `> 0.20` with `p1 < 0.90`, or minimum Mahalanobis distance exceeds `qchisq(0.975, d)` (Carstens et al. 2013).
* **Tier 4 Cluster-Wide `gdi` Governance (`cluster_propagated_gdi`):**
  Coalescent species delimitation via the genealogical divergence index ($\widehat{gdi} = 1 - e^{-2\tau/\theta}$) separates shallow population structure ($\widehat{gdi} < 0.20$) from isolated evolutionary lineages ($\widehat{gdi} > 0.70$; Jackson et al. 2017; Carstens et al. 2013). Rather than letting missing ploidy counts or distant allopatric polyploids block unsequenced local members, `propagate_cluster_gdi_governance()` aggregates `G1` and `G2` `coalescent_gdi` estimates per taxon and `coancestry_cluster` alongside regional (`<= 250 km`) `gmm_cluster` lineages while suppressing transfer strictly when observed cytotypes conflict inside the exact `coancestry_cluster` or local `2.0 km` deme alongside conflicts against the recipient's known ploidy. Admixed rows and evicted misidentified anchors alongside `tier0_abstention_flag == 1` vouchers stay excluded. Whenever `cluster_propagated_gdi < 0.20`, Stage 3 `Putative_Cryptic_Species` is blocked across unsequenced members (`[Cluster Coalescent Oversplit Block: propagated gdi=...]`). Conversely, when `cluster_propagated_gdi > 0.70` and `tier0_abstention_flag != 1` inside a validated lineage hull, unsequenced cluster members receive `[Candidate Member via Cluster-Propagated gdi=...]` corroboration alongside standard gathering and non-clinal gates.

### Quantitative Taxon-Complex Delimitation Within Phase 7 (`taxon_complex_discovery_utils.R`)

Within Phase 7, I also scale beyond single-species comparisons to delimit species complexes across the genus. At first glance, local ecotypes and distinct species complexes seem to require different analytical approaches, but that difference is superficial because both represent continuous divergence surfaces across morphospace and ecological landscapes.

* **Multimodal Pairwise Affinity Calculation (`step07_complex_affinity_utils.R`):**
  I quarantine passive heterotypic or uncertain-coordinate types alongside open/hybrid vouchers inside `select_taxon_anchors()`, exclude cartographic SSURGO and buffer fields in favor of smooth Step 05 indices (`lithic_outcrop_index`, `wetland_seepage_index`, `summer_moisture_deficit_idx`, `maritime_continentality_idx`), calculate pairwise morphological and fine-scale edaphic distances alongside climatic and active-anthesis phenological distances across all active taxa, shrink taxon centroids toward clean homotypic nomenclatural type baselines with weight $\gamma_k$, and incorporate an optional 4th genomic affinity stream (`compute_genomic_complex_affinity()`, which blends co-ancestry overlap alongside backbone clade matching plus coalescent `gdi` with exact 3-stream invariance when genomic inputs are absent).
* **Unsupervised Graph Modularity and Hierarchical Delimitation (`taxon_complex_discovery_utils.R`):**
  I discover species complexes through pure POSIX label partitioning paired with unsupervised graph modularity (Leiden and Louvain algorithms) and Ward.D2 hierarchical clustering, which resolves Taxon by GMM Morphotype subclusters when $N_{\text{taxa}} \le 2$ while restoring the caller's `.Random.seed` on exit.
* **Complex Discovery Plates and Neighbor Exports (`taxon_complex_discovery_utils.R`):**
  I export affinity networks and hierarchical dendrograms (`fig07b_taxon_complex_delimitation_network.png`) alongside pairwise distance summaries and neighbor lists (`empirical_taxon_complexes.csv`, `pairwise_taxon_affinity_matrix.csv`, `focal_auto_neighbors.txt`), which provides quantitative evidence for species complex boundaries.
